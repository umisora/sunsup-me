Alcohol-free drinks for parties.

Website for enjoying parties alcohol-free.

Introducing various alcohol-free drinks suitable for various scenes.

The public site is for someone asked to handle an office drinking party. After the workday, non-alcoholic drinks are chosen by scene, appearance, size, and taste.

What is published

Office party

It sums up what to serve after hours at an open office drinking party.

How to choose

  • Scene: something you can take to the next office party as it is.
  • Appearance: it does not look out of place on a meeting table.
  • Size: an amount one person can finish.
  • Taste: mix juice and soda, and include one bitter drink.

The list

The drink list is grouped into six categories: citrus and soda, grape and berry, juice and nectar, tea and herb, craft bottles, and cans for parties. It says the list contains 898 bottles, with photographed ones listed first. From the list, you pick one bottle per category.

After a meeting

Drinks to serve when a meeting ends. Mixing sweet and not-sweet is recommended, so people who dislike sweet drinks can drink them too. For autumn, grapes, pears, and apples are the focus, with one hojicha mixed in.
